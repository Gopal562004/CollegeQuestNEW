About

Established in 2008, St. John College of Engineering & Management is a premier Institute located in Maharashtra. The institute offers Degree and Diploma courses including 8 UG and 2 PG courses. Additionally, it also has After 10th Diploma courses. These programs are offered in Full Time mode. Candidates can pursue a range of courses like After 10th Diploma, MMS, MBA/PGDM, B.E. / B.Tech with streams in Engineering, Business & Management Studies.
